Quick start
  1. Force: pick the slot and number you want to force.
  2. Done (top right) saves and goes back to the calculator.
  3. Spectator types numbers. "=" shows the force once enough numbers are in.
  4. Hold = for 1 s to peek at what they entered.
Tap ? on any page for help with that page. Full guide: How to use, below.
Choosing what to force
  1. Slot: 15 slots. Whichever slot is showing when you tap Done becomes the active force.
  2. Force type:
    • Number: type the number "=" should show.
    • Date & time: pick the order, year and time, and type how many seconds ahead (e.g. 38 if your reveal is always 38 s after "="). The preview shows what it forces right now; the real value is worked out when "=" is pressed.
    • Real maths / book test: no force; optionally look the real total up in a list (page → word).
    • Days since birth: needs Birth day, month and year as data fields.
  3. Special slots: Replacement keeps the real total and only replaces its last digits (07;39;43 steps through a list). Confederate shows a small "+N" label for a helper to call out.
  4. Note: a reminder, shown when you switch to the slot with the secret selector.

What the spectator enters, and what you see or hear
  1. + Add field for each number you'll ask for, and pick what it is (Any number, Birth day, Age, Zip…). Use ↑ ↓ to reorder, ✕ to remove. Each number counts when they press an operator or "=". "=" shows the force once all are in; an earlier "=" just shows the real total.
  2. Birthday fields work out star sign, age, weekday and so on for the peek. Pick Spectator 2/3 for more people.
  3. Remainder (typed for them) goes last: "=" shows the subtotal, the ± key's slash tilts (left = tell them +, right = −), then every tap anywhere types the next digit until the total is exactly the force.
  4. Show in the peek / Speak to the earpiece: switch on what you want to see or hear.
These belong to the slot chosen on the Force page.

After the force shows, taps on 0, . and = are ignored until C/AC, so a peek can't spoil the number.

Clock hands: after the subtotal "=", the hand on the history clock that normally points up turns to the number of taps (1–12) and counts down as they tap. Mirrored clock (second hand on the other side) = tell them "−"; normal = "+". It snaps back when done.

With both quick-set and adhoc keys on: display at 0 picks the slot, otherwise it sets the adhoc number.

Seeing what they entered
  1. Set how long you hold each spot to open the peek (seconds). 0 = off. Use one you'll remember and switch the others off.
  2. Auto-hide on: the peek closes when you lift your finger. Off: it stays; tap to close, swipe left/right for star-sign traits.
  3. Traits first on 0: holding 0 opens straight on the traits page.
  4. What the peek shows is set per slot under Data, peek & speech items.
Hearing the details in an earpiece
  1. Pair a Bluetooth earpiece and turn the phone's silent switch off.
  2. Switch speech on. On the final "=", the slot's spoken items are read out.
  3. Hold − to replay (or stop). A short − tap while speaking pauses/resumes.
  4. Numbers above "read digit by digit" are read one digit at a time (easier to remember).
Looking numbers up
  1. 20 lists. One entry per line: number|text, with no spaces around |, e.g. 86|mention.
  2. Use a list from a data field (Page|4, or a field named exactly like the list) or from the force (Booktest|4).
  3. Name a list Celebrity, keyed by ddmm or mmdd (matching how you collect the birthday), to peek a celebrity born that day.
  4. Name one ending in City (e.g. USCity) keyed by zip code for the City peek item.
Wording for zodiac peeks
  1. One line per sign, Capricorn first: Name (Element)|positive traits|negative traits.
  2. Chinese zodiac, Rat first: Animal|traits.
  3. Edit freely, e.g. to translate or shorten.
Making it look exactly like your Calculator
  1. The built-in look uses screenshots of an iPhone Pro on iOS 26/27.
  2. On another phone: take a screenshot of its Calculator cleared, and one while typing (e.g. 11×24). Pick them below; they import straight away and a message confirms it. iPhone and Google Pixel layouts are recognised automatically (or set Keypad layout).
  3. After importing, the preview shows every key outlined in green with what it does, the number area in red and the top buttons in blue. If a label is wrong, change Keypad layout.
  4. Alignment check shows a real "264" with the live text in red over it. Send a screenshot of it if the number looks off.
  5. Sum line above a forced result: hidden by default, because the sum wouldn't add up to the force.

The idea

It looks and works like the iPhone Calculator. The spectator types numbers; when they press = after enough numbers, the answer is your force.

Before the show

  1. Force: choose a slot and type the number (or a date/time code).
  2. Data, peek & speech items: list what you'll ask for (e.g. Day, Month, Year). The count decides when the force appears.
  3. Tap Done.

Performing

  1. Have them enter each number with an operator (× or +) between them.
  2. "=" before the last number just shows the real total, so it's safe.
  3. After the last number, "=" shows the force.
  4. Hold = to peek at their entries, birthday details and so on.

Honest maths: Remainder

Add Remainder as the last data field. After the subtotal "=", glance at the history clock (top left): the hand that normally points up now points to how many taps are needed (1–12). If the clock looks mirrored (its other hand flipped to the other side), say "press −", otherwise "press +". Then every tap anywhere types the next digit and the hand counts down; "=" lands exactly on the force, and the maths checks out. (Settings → Secret controls → Remainder cue to use the ± slash instead.)

Secret controls

Hold AC 3 s for this menu · hold ± or % to switch slots · hold ÷ to show the force · type a number and hold + to force it once. More on the Secret controls page.

Like the real Calculator

C clears the number being typed, AC clears all, holding C or ⌫ clears all, "=" again repeats, % works like iOS, the clock button shows History, long-press the number to Copy/Paste.
